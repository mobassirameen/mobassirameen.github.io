About Me

I am an experimental high energy physicist working at the frontier of particle physics and machine learning. My research focuses on the CMS experiment at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) at CERN, Geneva, Switzerland. I am actively involved in Higgs physics, developing particle-physics taggers using novel machine learning techniques, searches for new physics beyond the Standard Model, and detector development for future collider experiments.

Research Interests

Higgs Physics

I have strong interest in pursuing research in the following areas:

  • Precision measurements of the Higgs boson, particularly measurements of its couplings to fermions and gauge bosons, and exploring possible deviations from the Standard Model.
  • Searches for physics beyond the Standard Model, with a particular interest in Higgs-sector extensions, CP violation, and other direct/indirect probes of new physics.

Also, I would like to pursue searches for invisible and exotic Higgs decays, which could provide connections to dark matter, hidden sectors, or other weakly interacting new particles. These studies naturally connect precision Higgs physics with broader searches for new physics at the LHC, allowing the Higgs boson to be used as a sensitive probe of physics beyond the Standard Model.

Machine Learning in HEP

Developing and applying cutting-edge machine learning techniques including Binary Classifier, Deep Neural Networks, Graph Neural Networks, Transformer-based neural network, and generative models for particle physics applications.

Detector R&D and Instrumentation

I have a strong interest in experimental detector development and instrumentation for future high-energy physics experiments, particularly in the context of the High-Luminosity LHC. My interests include radiation-tolerant silicon pixel detectors and monolithic CMOS sensor technologies, with particular experience in MALTA2 sensor characterization under X-ray irradiation. I am also interested in Phase-II detector upgrades, including the development, assembly, testing, qualification, characterization, and integration of pixel tracking detectors such as the Inner Tracker (ITk), as well as HGCAL detector technologies. More broadly, I am interested in the development of high-performance tracking and its sensor and readout technologies, calorimeter systems and its performance studies for challenging radiation and high-occupancy environments.
