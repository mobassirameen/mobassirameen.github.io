About Me

I am a researcher in Experimental Particle Physics working on the CMS experiment at CERN. My research focuses on precision measurements of the Higgs boson, top-quark physics, machine learning applications, and future detector technologies for the High-Luminosity LHC.

Research Interests

Higgs Physics

Precision measurements of Higgs boson production and coupling to top quarks.

Machine Learning

Deep Learning and Transformer models for signal-background classification.

Detector R&D

Silicon pixel detectors, radiation studies and future collider upgrades.
